ANRI Fundamental Principles v0.1

Principle 1

ANRI is model-independent.

No specific AI model is considered permanent.

Principle 2

Engineering reasoning is more important than generation.

ANRI must explain decisions.

Principle 3

Every design must have:

assumptions

constraints

evaluation

traceability

Principle 4

Knowledge must be cumulative.

Every experiment contributes to future capability.

Principle 5

Human expertise remains part of the loop.
