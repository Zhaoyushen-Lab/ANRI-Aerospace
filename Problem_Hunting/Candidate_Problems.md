# CP001: Multimodal HAUV Configuration Optimization


## Source

Paper:

P001_HAUV_Review_2022

Section:

Open Problems and Research Challenges


## Problem Statement

How can a multimodal HAUV configuration be optimized
to achieve a balance between:

- aerial efficiency
- underwater capability
- cross-domain transition reliability?


## Background

Existing HAUV designs often optimize specific operation modes.

Examples:

- Fixed-wing HAUV:
  - high aerial efficiency
  - difficult transition


- Multi-rotor HAUV:
  - stable transition
  - limited endurance


## Current Limitation

A unified optimization methodology
for multimodal HAUV configuration is still insufficient.


## Potential ANRI Value

This problem requires integration of:

- aerodynamics
- hydrodynamics
- structural design
- mission requirements

# CP002: Time-varying Hydrodynamics During Transition


## Source

Paper:

P001_HAUV_Review_2022

Section:

Open Problems and Research Challenges


## Problem Statement

How do hydrodynamic characteristics change
during the air-water transition process?


## Background

During cross-domain movement:

- environmental medium changes
- forces change
- dynamic parameters vary


## Current Limitation

The time-varying hydrodynamic behavior
is not fully understood.


## Potential ANRI Value

Requires integration of:

- physics modeling
- simulation
- experimental knowledge

# CP003: Robust Cross-domain Motion Control


## Source

Paper:

P001_HAUV_Review_2022.md


## Problem Statement

How can HAUV maintain stable motion
during cross-domain operation
under uncertain environments?


## Challenges

- nonlinear dynamics
- changing system parameters
- wind disturbance
- wave disturbance


## Current Limitation

Many control methods remain limited
to simulation or simplified conditions.


## Potential ANRI Value

Potential integration of:

- dynamic modeling
- control theory
- adaptive reasoning
