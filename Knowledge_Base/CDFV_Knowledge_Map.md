# Cross-Domain Flight Vehicle Knowledge Map

Version: v0.1

Purpose:
Establish the initial knowledge architecture for ANRI-CDFV.

---

# 1. System Overview

Cross-domain flight vehicles are complex systems involving multiple physical domains.

Core domains:

- Aerodynamics
- Hydrodynamics
- Structures
- Propulsion
- Control
- Materials
- Artificial Intelligence

---

# 2. Physics Knowledge Layer

## 2.1 Aerodynamics

Purpose:

Understand vehicle behavior in air domain.

Key concepts:

- Lift
- Drag
- Airfoil
- Boundary layer
- Flow separation
- CFD simulation


## 2.2 Hydrodynamics

Purpose:

Understand underwater or water-entry behavior.

Key concepts:

- Buoyancy
- Drag in water
- Water impact
- Free surface interaction


## 2.3 Structural Mechanics

Purpose:

Ensure structural reliability.

Key concepts:

- Stress
- Strain
- Fatigue
- Lightweight structures
