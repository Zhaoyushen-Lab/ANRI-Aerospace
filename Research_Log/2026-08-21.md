# Research Log 2026-08-21

## Project Evolution

Today marks the transition from ANRI-Aerospace to ANRI-CDFV.

The initial idea of ANRI was to explore how AI can enhance aerospace research workflows.

After analyzing the characteristics of cross-domain flight vehicle design, the project focus has been refined toward:

AI-Native Research Infrastructure for Cross-Domain Flight Vehicle Design (ANRI-CDFV).

---

## Background

Cross-domain flight vehicles involve multiple engineering disciplines, including:

- Aerodynamics
- Hydrodynamics
- Structures
- Propulsion
- Control systems

Traditional engineering development relies heavily on fragmented expert knowledge, separated simulation tools, and manual design iteration.

This creates challenges in knowledge integration and engineering evolution.

---

## Current Hypothesis

Future aerospace engineering may require a new research paradigm:

Knowledge → Design → Simulation → Evaluation → Knowledge Update

AI should not only assist individual engineering tasks, but also help build a continuously evolving engineering research infrastructure.

---

## Current Focus

The first stage of ANRI-CDFV focuses on:

1. Building a cross-domain flight vehicle knowledge base.
2. Exploring Knowledge Agent prototypes.
3. Investigating AI-assisted engineering workflows.

---

## Current Questions

1. How can AI organize multidisciplinary engineering knowledge?
2. How can engineering experience be converted into reusable knowledge?
3. How can AI accelerate the design iteration process of complex flight systems?
