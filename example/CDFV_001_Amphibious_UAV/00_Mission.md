# CDFV-001 Mission Definition


## Objective

Design a conceptual amphibious UAV
for maritime surveillance.


## Mission Scenario

The aircraft should:

- operate from water surface
- perform aerial reconnaissance
- maintain long endurance
- minimize operational cost


## Initial Constraints

Environment:

Marine environment

Mission:

Surveillance

Design Stage:

Conceptual design

## Design Philosophy

The purpose of this case is not to finalize a production aircraft, but to validate the ANRI engineering reasoning workflow from mission definition to concept evaluation.
