Paper_001_HAUV_Review

Core Problem:
Cross-domain mobility between air and water.

Why Important:
Existing UAV and AUV systems are limited to single environments.

Main Challenges:

1. Multi-physics coupling
   - Aerodynamics
   - Hydrodynamics

2. Transition dynamics
   - Water entry
   - Water exit

3. Control uncertainty
   - Nonlinear
   - Time-varying
   - Disturbance


Vehicle Categories:

1. Bio-inspired HAUV

2. Fixed-wing HAUV

3. Multi-rotor HAUV

4. Multimodal HAUV


ANRI-CDFV Connection:

This paper defines the first engineering knowledge domain:
"Cross-domain vehicle architecture and transition problems"
