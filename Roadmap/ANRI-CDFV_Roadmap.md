# ANRI-CDFV Roadmap

## Vision

Develop an AI-Native Research Infrastructure for Cross-Domain Flight Vehicle Design.

The goal is to explore how AI can connect multidisciplinary knowledge, simulation tools, engineering design and iterative improvement.

---

## Version History

### ANRI-Aerospace v0.1
Initial exploration of AI-native aerospace research workflow.

### ANRI-CDFV v0.1
Focus shifted toward cross-domain flight vehicle design as the first validation scenario.

---

## Development Plan

### Phase 1: Knowledge Infrastructure

- Build aerospace and cross-domain vehicle knowledge base
- Organize research papers and engineering concepts
- Develop Knowledge Agent prototype


### Phase 2: Engineering Workflow Integration

- Connect AI with design and simulation tools
- Explore AI-assisted conceptual design


### Phase 3: Engineering Evolution Loop

- Establish feedback loop:
Knowledge → Design → Simulation → Evaluation → Knowledge Update
